### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a passionate programmer and bodybuilder now based in the UK. I live for the excitement of coding—understanding a vision, solving problems, and bringing ideas to life through functional, scalable applications. My journey began with a Bachelor’s in Computer Science, where I fell in love with programming while mastering React and Next.js.

I launched my career as a Front-End Developer at Tentwenty in the UAE, designing intuitive user interfaces that enhanced client project success. Later, I stepped into a Full-Stack Web Developer role at Silkhaus, also in the UAE, sharpening my Laravel skills while building robust web solutions that streamlined property tech operations. Alongside those roles, I freelanced for global clients, delivering creative fixes to real-world challenges.

In late 2024, I relocated to the UK to embark on a new chapter as a full-time freelancer. After years of working for companies, I signed up on Upwork to take control of my career and pursue projects on my own terms. Now, as a Senior Laravel Developer with deep expertise in Vue.js, React, and Next.js, I’m ready to tackle ambitious challenges and create impactful solutions. Let’s connect and build something extraordinary together!
